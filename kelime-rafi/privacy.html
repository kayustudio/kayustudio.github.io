Privacy Policy

İngilizce Kelime Rafı · Effective date: November 19, 2026 · Türkçe

İngilizce Kelime Rafı ("the app") is developed and published by Kayu Studio ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and the choices you have.

Summary

  • No accounts, no sign-in. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts or photos.
  • We do not collect any personal data. The app has no analytics, no crash reporting and no advertising.
  • There are no ads and no in-app purchases.
  • We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and the app does not use Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • We do not sell or share any data.

Information we collect

None. The app does not send any information to us or to third parties. It works fully offline; word images and all learning content are bundled with the app.

Information that stays on your device

The name you enter, your level test result, the words you know and review, grammar and vocabulary scores, your daily streak and your settings are stored only in a local database on your device. Deleting the app, or using "Baştan başla" (Start over) in Settings, removes them.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app schedules it directly on your device. No data is sent to us for this. You can turn the reminder off in the app's Settings or in your phone's settings at any time.

Text-to-speech

Words and sentences are read aloud by your phone's built-in text-to-speech feature. The app does not send this text to us.

Data retention

We do not hold any of your data. Data on your device stays until you delete the app or reset your progress.

Your rights

Because we do not collect or process personal data, we hold nothing to access, correct or delete. You can still contact us with any question at kayustudio.support@gmail.com.

Children

The app is suitable for learners of all ages and does not collect personal data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows the latest version.
