About the app

İngilizce Kelime Rafı is an English vocabulary app for Turkish speakers. Start with a level test, then study at your level with word cards, reviews, weak words, grammar lessons and tests, phrasal verbs and fill-in-the-blank questions.

Frequently asked

Where is my progress saved?
Only on your device. There are no accounts or servers. Deleting the app deletes your progress; it is not transferred to a new phone.

Are there ads or purchases?
No. The app is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases. See the Privacy Policy.
