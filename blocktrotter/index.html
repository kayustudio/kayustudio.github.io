Blocktrotter

Slide the blocks, free the red one — across five worlds and 500 levels.

Blocks slide only along their length: horizontal blocks left and right, vertical blocks up and down. Clear a path and drive the red block out through the gate. Every level is solvable, and every one needs a little planning — sometimes you have to move one block just to make room for another. Travel from a Japanese garden through an Egyptian desert, a tropical island and arctic glaciers to a neon city.

Frequently asked questions

How do I play?

Drag a block to slide it. Get the red block to the gate on the right side of the board. A short guided tutorial runs the first time you play; you can replay it from Settings › How to Play.

I'm stuck. What can I do?

Tap the light bulb for a hint: it plays the best next move. You start with 3 hints and can get another by watching a short video ad. Undo takes back your last move and Restart resets the level.

How do stars work?

Solve a level in the fewest possible moves for 3 stars. A few extra moves still earns 2 stars, and any solve earns at least 1.

Where is my progress saved?

Only on your device. Deleting the app deletes your unlocked levels, stars and hints.

Are there ads or purchases?

The game is free and shows ads (Google AdMob). There are no in-app purchases. See the Privacy Policy.

Which languages are supported?

English and Turkish. The game follows your device language; you can switch it in Settings.
